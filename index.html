Ask a Question
What is RPM Health Rewards?

RPM Health Rewards is a remote patient monitoring engagement platform concept that combines provider operational visibility with a patient-facing experience for assigned health metrics, progress feedback, and qualifying outcome rewards.

Who can use the Provider Portal?

The Provider Portal is intended for authorized clinical, operational, and billing-workflow users within participating provider organizations. Access requirements are determined by the organization’s implementation and security controls.

How do patients access the Patient Portal?

Patients use a clinic-assigned PIN to access their monitoring plan, submit their assigned reading, and view recent activity. On compatible devices, the Patient Portal may also be installed as a Progressive Web App from the browser.

Can the Patient Portal be installed on a phone?

Yes. On supported Android browsers, the Patient Portal may show an Install option. On iPhone or iPad, open the portal in Safari, select Share, then choose Add to Home Screen.

How do rewards work?

In the current prototype, qualifying Blood Pressure and Weight readings that meet configured targets can generate a recorded $5 reward transaction. HbA1c and BMI readings are logged but are not currently reward-enabled.

Does clicking a billing action submit an insurance claim?

No. The current Provider Portal supports an internal billing workflow such as Ready for Review, Under Review, Ready to Bill, or Billed. It does not submit, adjudicate, or verify payer claims.

Is this website providing medical advice?

No. RPM Health Rewards is an operational and patient-engagement platform concept. Individual clinical decisions, patient care, device selection, documentation, and billing determinations remain the responsibility of qualified providers and participating organizations.
